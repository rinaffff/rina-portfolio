About / 視点

I construct visual worlds
with obsessive intention.

I'm an Art Director who gets genuinely excited by things that usually get overlooked—the weight of paper, the quiet rhythm between text blocks, or how lighting in a 3D environment changes the emotional tone of a brand. I don't believe in generic branding or template-driven design.

"I like combining raw energy with strict structural precision. It’s about creating work that feels alive, refined, and impossible to scroll past."

My background spans brand strategy, full-scale shoot production, SketchUp spatial modeling, and high-editorial monograph design. Whether directing a camera crew on set or refining kerning on a 400-page book, my focus is always on clarity, character, and visual longevity.
